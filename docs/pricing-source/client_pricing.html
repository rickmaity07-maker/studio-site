How it works and when you pay

You never pay for something you haven't seen. I never build without a commitment. Both sides are covered at every step.

  1. Free mock-upA design of your homepage within 2 to 3 days. If you don't like it, we stop there and you owe nothing.
    You pay nothing
  2. Short written agreementOne page: what's included, the price, the timeline, two rounds of changes, and what counts as extra.
    30% deposit to start the build
  3. Build on a private preview linkYou can open the site on your phone while it's being built and send feedback any time. Usually 1 to 2 weeks for a website, 2 to 4 for a shop.
  4. Review and changesTwo rounds of changes are included. You approve the final version.
    Remaining 70% on approval
  5. Go live and handoverThe site goes live on your domain the same day. You get admin logins, a short video on how to edit, and the files. Everything is yours.
  6. 30 days of free fixesAnything that doesn't work as agreed is fixed free for 30 days after launch.

Online shops are paid in three parts: 30% to start, 40% when the shop works on the preview link, 30% on approval. Payment by bank transfer or a local payment method; you get an invoice for every payment.

Questions

I already have Instagram. Why do I need a website?

Google shows websites first when people search for places to eat. A site gives them your menu, hours and location in one tap, works for people without Instagram, and sends them to your Instagram too.

Can I change the menu and prices myself?

Yes. Every site comes with an admin panel. You log in on your phone or laptop and edit text, prices and photos. No coding.

Do I have to pay every month?

No. The website is a one-off price. Care plans are optional, for people who want hosting, backups and changes handled for them.

Who owns the website?

You do. The domain is registered in your name, and after the final payment you get all logins and files.

What if I don't like the design?

The first mock-up is free and comes with no obligation. During the build you get two rounds of changes, and you see every version on a preview link.

Can the site take orders or bookings?

Yes. It can link to your existing booking and delivery services, or include an online shop with payments.

Can the site be in more than one language?

Yes. Sites can switch between languages, for example English and your local language.
