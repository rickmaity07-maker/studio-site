## What it means
- **Rich markets (USA, UK, Canada, Australia, Germany):** you are 30-60% cheaper than typical freelancers.
  Good as a hook ("custom design + admin portal for less than a template agency"), but you could charge more:
  roughly US$1,500, £1,200, CA$1,800, A$2,000, €1,000 and still be at the low end of the market.
- **Southern / Eastern Europe, India, Africa:** your prices sit inside the normal local range. Keep them.
- Online shops are priced low almost everywhere; local market shops usually start at 2-4x a normal site.

To change a price, edit `PRICES` in `scripts/pricing.py`, then run `python scripts/pricing.py`.
